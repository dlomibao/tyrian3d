Tyrian 3D docs

How the remastered art, models and music are made and checked.

  • Art remastering and 3D modelling: from a sprite to a model in the game, concept images, feature lists, review sheets, the grade → fix → regrade loop, and the other remastered art (pictures, backgrounds, effects).
  • Music and sound remastering: from the original FM songs to the Synth and Orchestral versions, and the remade sound effects and voices.

Each page starts with the most interesting part: the problems we ran into and how we solved them, from calibrating an AI grader against a person's ratings to recording the songs straight from the game's own FM player.

The decisions behind each step are in DECISIONS.md; credits are in CREDITS.md. Everything here is our own work based on the original game's art and music; the original game data (tyrian21.zip) is never committed (D3, D41).
